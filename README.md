# Paytm BizMate

**Your AI teammate for business.**

Paytm BizMate is a hackathon project for a conversational AI teammate that helps merchants complete business tasks, verify outcomes, and track unresolved work.

## Hackathon MVP

- **Settlement Investigation:** investigate a settlement shortfall, explain the evidence, prepare a support case, and track resolution.
- **Customer Win-back:** identify inactive customers with eligible consent, prepare an offer, obtain approval, simulate sending, and verify delivery.
- **Capability-gap handling:** recognise unsupported or unauthorised requests and hand off unfinished work honestly.

The MVP is specified for Track 3, Autonomous AI Teammates, at the Paytm Build for India AI Hackathon 2026 in Hyderabad.
