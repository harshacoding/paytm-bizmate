Business model

Start with service economics.
Earn a merchant growth product.

Phase 1 · Internal buyer

Merchant support

Value is eligible cases handled faster, with fewer repeat contacts. Compare total cost, including model calls and human review, against the current process.

Phase 2 · Merchant option

Growth add-on

If win-back experiments produce measurable net contribution, offer campaign planning and measurement to merchants on a paid plan.

Contribution hypothesis = cases assisted × net minutes saved × support cost per minute − operating and review cost.

Pricing, merchant uptake, retention lift, and support savings require a live pilot.
